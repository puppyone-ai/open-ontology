One `cdc-transaction-v1` stream, one source, lineage, epoch, and transaction, is written to a new private directory. Normalized cells stay opaque: null, unavailable, and distinct values keep their spelling. Ordinals `0..=32767` are checked by a custom deserializer. `32768`, `65535`, and `-1` return `ordinal_range`. `END` checks collection counts, the full unique sequence set, and an explicit commit LSN. Rollback writes `rolled_back\n` and does not write `SEAL` or `manifest.json`.

Chunk files are written, flushed, and `sync_all`'d, then renamed. The manifest records ordered chunk hashes, counts, the source binding, first and last sequence, and the commit position. `SEAL` is written only after the chunks, the manifest, and the parent directory have been synced. Recovery yields an iterator only after the seal, the manifest, and each regular non-symlink chunk path, length, and hash agree. A missing end, a missing chunk, a short file, or a bad checksum stays incomplete or unknown and is never acknowledged.
