The crate walk writes source/CRATE-SHA256SUMS first. The evidence walk then writes SHA256SUMS and includes the crate manifest. The walker skips its output path, so SHA256SUMS itself is absent from SHA256SUMS.
Host files written after the walker returns are absent from SHA256SUMS:

- linux/inventory/console.log
- linux/inventory/hostconfig.txt
- linux/inventory/mounts.txt
- linux/inventory/docker-run.exit
- linux/inventory/container-removed.txt
- cleanup.log

cleanup.sh is in the walk. cleanup.log is produced when owned resources are removed.
