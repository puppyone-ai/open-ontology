The crate walk writes source/CRATE-SHA256SUMS first. The evidence walk then writes SHA256SUMS at the rerun root and includes the crate manifest. The walker skips its output path, so SHA256SUMS itself is absent from SHA256SUMS.

Host files written after the walker returns are absent from SHA256SUMS:

- linux/inventory/console.log
- linux/inventory/hostconfig.txt
- linux/inventory/mounts.txt
- linux/inventory/docker-run.exit
- linux/inventory/container-removed.txt
- cleanup.log

`linux/inventory/capacity.txt`, `image.txt`, and `invocation.txt` are written before the container starts, so the walker includes them.

cleanup.sh is in the walk when it is present before the walker starts. cleanup.log is produced when owned resources are removed.
